# Memoir Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)

A secure, 100% on-device browser extension that brings **1-tap autofill** and **smart credential auto-capture** to Memoir Personal Vault.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Chrome, Brave, or Edge (Free & 1-Minute Setup)

1. Open your Chromium browser (Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Arc, or Opera).
2. Go to `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions` in Edge).
3. In the top-right corner, turn **ON** the **Developer mode** toggle switch.
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select this `extension` folder (`personalvault/extension`).
6. The **Memoir Vault & Autofill** extension is now installed! Pin it to your browser toolbar.

---

## 🔐 How to Log In & Use

1. Click the **Memoir Rhino icon** (🦏) in your browser toolbar.
2. Enter your **4-Digit Memoir Account Code** (e.g. `2002` for Maaz, `2005` for Deepti).
3. Click **Connect Vault**.
4. You're all set!

---

## ⚡ Features

- **1-Tap In-Field Autofill**: When you focus on username, password, or card fields on any website (e.g. GitHub, Amazon, Netflix), click the Memoir dropdown to autofill your credentials instantly.
- **Smart Form Capture**: When you submit a login or registration form on any website, Memoir asks *"Save to Memoir?"* and saves it with domain provenance.
- **Vault Data Isolation**: Captured browser credentials are automatically tagged with `source: 'Chrome Extension'` and appear in the Memoir Web App under **Memories $\rightarrow$ Browser Captures**.
